## Questions to ask them (pick 2 or 3)

Aim one at the most senior person. Avoid anything you could Google.

**Strategic (best for the hiring manager or regional lead)**
1. "Inhaled anesthesia has been under pressure globally this year. How does the Indonesia team see Suprane's role in the portfolio over the next 3 years: growth, defend, or manage for profit?"
2. "What did the 2025 drop look like from the inside? Was it more demand or more supply?" (Only if they haven't already answered it in Q&A.)
3. "Where does Indonesia sit in the region's priorities for the hospital portfolio, and what would make it get more investment?"

**Role (best for the hiring manager)**
4. "What would make someone in this role a clear success at the 12-month mark?"
5. "How is the PM's relationship with the sales team set up here? Shared targets, or influence only?"

**Culture (best for HR)**
6. "With the company focused on continuous improvement, what's one thing that's changed in how the Indonesia team works in the last year?"

**Close with:** "Is there anything in my plan or background you'd like me to clarify before we finish?" This invites the hidden objection while you can still answer it.
