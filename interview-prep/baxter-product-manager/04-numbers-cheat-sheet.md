## Reality check: 20 bottles per vaporizer per month
Rough rule of thumb (verify with medical): desflurane at 1 L/min fresh gas flow and ~6% uses roughly 15-20 mL liquid an hour. A 240 mL bottle then lasts **~12-14 hours** at low flow, ~7 hours at 2 L/min.
- 20 bottles = **140 to 280 anesthesia hours a month** per vaporizer.
- At 22 working days, that's **6 to 13 hours of desflurane a day** on one machine.
- That is very high. It works only if the vaporizer sits in a busy OR used for most cases.
- **Better answer:** "20 is the target for qualified high-volume ORs. I'd rather set 12 to 15 for month 1-3 and use the Q3 usage check to decide go/no-go." Showing you stress-tested your own number earns more trust than defending it.
