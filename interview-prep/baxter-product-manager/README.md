## The 5 things that matter most

1. **$1.5M at 35% share needs a 19% market recovery.** Commit to revenue. Calendar 2026 ≈ $1.3M, exit run-rate $1.5M+.
2. **Impacts on slides 9-10 are annualized.** Phased by launch date, 2026 incremental is about $390k.
3. **Budget is $50k, not $45k.** ROI is about 3.7x gross margin in year one (assuming 60% margin).
4. **Have a view on the EU desflurane restriction and Baxter's 2026 supply constraints.** Responsible use, low-flow, and a stop rule.
5. **Know C and O in 60 seconds each.** The portfolio link: parenteral nutrition protocols call for daily vitamins, so bundle O with C.
