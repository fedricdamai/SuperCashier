## A. Opening and case choice

### A1. Why did you pick Suprane instead of C (multivitamin) or O (parenteral nutrition)?
*Likely asked by:* Hiring manager · *Level:* **Must nail**

**What they're testing:** Portfolio judgment. Did you dodge the biggest product?

**How to approach:**
- Show you looked at all three in one sentence each.
- Give your selection criteria: size of the problem, how much a PM can change it, strategic risk.
- Offer a 60-second view on C and O. Don't launch into it unless they say yes.

**Model answer:**
> I looked at all three. C is the market leader at 67% share and still gaining. Its job is defending against copies without starting a price war. O is flat at 36%, held back by central-only access and fish-oil familiarity. Suprane lost 40% in one year. That's where a product manager changes the outcome the most, and it's the hardest of the three. I'd rather show you how I think on the burning platform. I have a short view on C and O if that's useful.

**Trap to avoid:** Saying 'because it's interesting' or sounding like you avoided C because it's big.

### A2. Give me your whole plan in 60 seconds.
*Likely asked by:* Any · *Level:* **Must nail**

**What they're testing:** Can you compress? Executives think in one-liners.

**How to approach:**
- Problem, diagnosis, where to play, 3 moves, money, target, the ask.
- Use numbers. No adjectives.
- Practise until it's under 60 seconds with a timer.

**Model answer:**
> Suprane fell 40% in 2025, from 1.5 to 0.9 million. The market fell 35% and we lost share on top. Two causes: hospitals without vaporizers can't use it, and sustainability concerns are growing. So three moves. Protect the 0.9 million base with an honest green-anesthesia program. Accelerate use in vaporizer-ready, high-volume accounts by focusing on long, obese and elderly cases where fast emergence matters. Unlock six Jakarta hospitals with a vaporizer access program, with a go/no-go in Q3. Fifty thousand dollars. Calendar 2026 at about 1.3 million, exiting the year at a 1.5 million run-rate.

**Trap to avoid:** Narrating slides in order instead of telling the story.

### A3. What is the single biggest reason Suprane declined in 2025?
*Likely asked by:* Regional marketing · *Level:* **Must nail**

**What they're testing:** Root-cause discipline. Do you separate market effect from brand effect?

**How to approach:**
- Split it: market fell 35%, Suprane fell 40%. Most of the drop is the market.
- Name your top hypothesis for the 5-point underperformance: access.
- Admit the data is outside-in and say how you'd validate. Then ask what they saw.

**Model answer:**
> Most of it is the market. The implied market fell 35%, Suprane fell 40%, so about seven-eighths of our drop is the category, and the rest is us. A 35% drop in a procedure-driven category is unusual, because surgeries didn't fall 35%. So before I'd trust it I'd check sell-in versus sell-out, tender timing, distributor stock, and supply. Baxter reported inhaled-anesthesia declines and supply constraints this year, so part of this may be supply, not demand. If it's supply, the fix is S&OP, not marketing. I'm curious what you saw internally.

**Trap to avoid:** Picking one cause with total confidence when your data says 'based on assumption'.
