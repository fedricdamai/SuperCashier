# 05. Leadership and Behavioral Prep

I don't know your CV, so this file gives you the frames and the prompts. **Fill in the blanks tonight.** Five stories cover almost every behavioral question a senior panel asks.

## The format: STAR, weighted for seniority
- **S**ituation: 1 sentence. Where, what was at stake, one number.
- **T**ask: 1 sentence. What *you* owned.
- **A**ction: 60% of the answer. 3 things *you* did. Use "I".
- **R**esult: a number, then what you learned.

Under 2 minutes. End with the lesson, not the result. The lesson is what makes it sound senior.

## Likely leadership questions for a managerial-track PM

| Question | Which story | Angle |
|---|---|---|
| Tell me about a time you turned something around. | 1 | Diagnose before acting |
| Tell me about a time you failed. | 2 | Ownership and learning |
| How do you handle disagreement with sales leadership? | 3 | Ask why, then test |
| Tell me about working with medical or regulatory. | 4 | Partners, not gatekeepers |
| How do you develop people? | 5 | Stretch, fast feedback, exposure |
| How do you prioritise with limited budget? | Use the 50% cut answer (question E2) | Protect base, fund certainty, shrink the bet |
| How do you make decisions with incomplete data? | Use the Suprane root-cause answer (A3) | Hypothesis, validate fast, pre-set stop rules |
| Where do you see yourself in 5 years? | None | Leading a portfolio or BU, built on owning P&L outcomes here |

## Motivation answers (personalise)

**Why Baxter?**
Something specific. For example: Baxter's products are in the OR and ICU when patients are most fragile. The role spans anesthesia, nutrition and vitamins, so it's a portfolio job. And the company is in a turnaround phase under new leadership, which is when good product managers make the most visible difference.

**Why this role?**
The Indonesian hospital market mixes JKN cost pressure, tenders and fast-growing private hospital groups. A PM here has to think like a strategist and execute like a KAM. That's the job you want.

**Why now?**
Your next step. Tie it to owning a plan end to end, budget included, which this case let you practise.

## Phrases that signal seniority
- "My hypothesis is... and here's how I'd test it."
- "I'd set the stop rule now, before we're emotionally invested."
- "That's a fair challenge. I'd revise it to..."
- "The constraint isn't budget, it's field-force time."
- "Revenue is what I commit to. Share is the outcome."

## Phrases to drop
- "I'm passionate about..." (show it, don't say it)
- "Basically..." / "To be honest..."
- "We did..." when you mean "I did..."
- "It's a win-win."
