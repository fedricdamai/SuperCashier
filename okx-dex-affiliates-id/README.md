## How scoring works

Five 1-5 scores, weighted: **Volume potential 30%, Trading intent 25%, Onchain readiness 15%, Reachability 15%, Risk safety 15%.** Score = weighted sum / 5 x 100.

Three gates override the math:
- Active legal overhang (risk = 1) or an airdrop-led audience forces **Low**.
- **High** needs verified evidence and risk of 3 or better. Unverified leads cap at Medium.
- Licensed exchanges and their academies (Indodax, Pintu, Tokocrypto, Reku, Upbit, Bybit Indonesia, Pluang, Ajaib) are left out. They fight for the same trader.

Change any weight or threshold on the Method sheet and the whole list re-ranks.

## Four findings that shaped the ranking

1. **Traders left memecoins for perps.** Pump.fun activity fell ~80% by June 2026 as traders moved to perps ([Pluang](https://pluang.com/en/news-feed/aktivitas-pumpfun-turun-80-persen-trader-tinggalkan-memecoin-drag-fee-solana)). Perps communities rank above memecoin call channels.
2. **Every paid creator now needs a certificate.** POJK 6/2026 requires certification for anyone giving crypto information, and makes firms answerable for their marketing partners ([Okezone](https://economy.okezone.com/read/2026/07/08/320/3228975/ojk-wajibkan-sertifikasi-influencer-kripto-ini-tujuannya)). The AFTECH x IARFC certification cohort ranks as a sourcing channel for that reason.
3. **The biggest audience on the list is on hold.** Akademi Crypto (Timothy Ronald, Kalimasada) would top the volume score. Police halted the fraud inquiry in October 2026, but a complainant asked for a special review ([Justisio](https://justisio.com/berita/2026-10-26-polda-metro-hentikan-penyelidikan-laporan-dugaan-penipuan-kripto-timot)). Revisit after it closes.
4. **Get a legal read before any paid deal.** Law 4/2026 lets OJK block offshore platforms serving Indonesians ([HSF Kramer](https://www.hsfkramer.com/ru/notes/indonesia/2026-posts/indonesia-reshapes-digital-asset-and-cryptocurrency-framework-with-2026-financial-services-omnibus-law)). I found no OJK licence for OKX.

## Check these before you submit

- Direct page fetches were blocked in the research environment. Follower counts come from third-party trackers (socialcounts, Heepsy) and from Indodax Academy profiles, which are an exchange's content marketing. Open each top-8 link and re-check the numbers on the platform itself.
- Michael Wyann's ~100K comes from a Cryptonews Indonesia listicle. Two search summaries described his channel differently, so watch a few videos before relying on it.
- The Hyperliquid Indonesia organiser may be a fan group, not Hyperliquid Labs. Confirm who runs it.
- OKX DEX referral tier tables differ across OKX regional help pages. Confirm the live rate in the referral dashboard before quoting anything.
- 28 rows are marked **Lead**: a name from a directory or panel, nothing more.
