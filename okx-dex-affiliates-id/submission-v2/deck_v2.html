Speaker notes · slide 1

What OKX Wallet and DEX offer, and what each earns. OKX DEX charges an interface fee by token group: 0.1% between majors and stablecoins (Group 1), 0.25% for mid-caps (Group 2), 0.5% when Group 1 or 2 is swapped against any other token, which covers most memecoin buys with SOL or USDC (OKX notice, effective 12 May 2026; swaps between two "other" tokens stay at 0%). Meme Mode itself launched fee-free in July 2024, so the 0.5% is recent and worth confirming on the day. Tokenized stocks (xStocks) moved to the Group 2 rate on 7 Sep 2026. Earn carries no interface fee, and perps opened on Hyperliquid through OKX Wallet's dApp connection pay Hyperliquid, not OKX. One thing to watch: Hyperliquid lets wallets add a "builder code" fee (Phantom has earned about $20.6M this way); if OKX Wallet adds one, perp communities move up our list.

How we measure every partner: FTDs (first-time depositors through the partner's link) first, then trades a day; swaps and revenue follow from those two. A retail FTD trades on about 20% of days, 8 trades of about $25, so $40 a day or $1.2K a month; a power trader (the partner's clan, FnF or referral-book traders) trades on 70% of days, 20 trades of about $120, so about $1,680 a day or $50K a month, the same volume as 42 FTDs. These are assumptions anchored on Indonesian data: an active OKX Wallet Boost user averaged about $550 a day (@AiSlimx, May 2026), a KOL trader did about $170K in a month on Trojan (@Ponyin, Mar 2026), and creator referral links with no incentive turn about 0.07% of followers into traders (Trojan data, @0xSarimin).

Revenue per trader at OKX's published fee: a trencher $6 a month and a power trencher about $252; DeFi swaps $900 × ~0.15% = $1.35; Boost and tokenized stocks $600 × ~0.3% = $1.80; Easy-mode majors $300 × 0.1% = $0.30. So most BD time goes to trenchers, and every partner onboards 5 power traders in its first 3 days before posting for FTDs, because power traders carry about 90% of swaps. Fee upside: OKX DEX earned about $4.4M in Oct 2026 (DefiLlama), an average take of about 0.09%; at published fees our 8 partners' first-month swaps ($1.77M) would generate about $7.7K before the partner's share, against $707 at the brief's 0.04%. Speaker notes · slide 3

Calculation (brief): month-1 contribution = volume × 0.04% − fixed fee − revenue share × revenue. On their terms: A $4M × 0.04% = $1,600 − $2,000 − $320 = $720 short; B $2M × 0.04% = $800 − $280 = +$520; C $6M × 0.04% = $2,400 − $3,000 − $240 = $840 short. Their fixed fees only pay back above $6.25M a month for A and $8.33M for C.

Why B: it is the only offer that is positive on its own terms, its traders already trade onchain repeatedly (the brief's goal), and with no fixed fee there is nothing to recover if volume disappoints.

Pilot structure for B: 60 days, revenue share only, 25% base rising to 35% once at least 50 referred wallets trade on 3 or more days and at least 30% are still active on day 30; 30-day clawback for wash trading, self-referral and sybil wallets; 25-35% sits inside OKX's DEX referral tiers. If they ask why we cut the partner we want from 35% to 25%: we don't, we tie their 35% to proof. They reach it the moment 50 wallets trade on 3+ days, and if that happens in month 1, month 1 is +$520 instead of +$600, a trade we're happy to make.

At 50% volume: $1M × 0.04% = $400; our 25% leaves OKX +$300 and its own 35% leaves +$260, so B stays positive for OKX. For B itself, the payout halves too: $200 to $100 on our terms, $280 to $140 on its ask. That's the moment B is most likely to ask for a fixed fee, and the answer is the step-up, not a fee.

A and C: the brief allows one pilot at first, so they wait; later, revenue share only, with a fixed fee back on the table in month 3 if 90 days of data supports it. A is airdrop-led, so its volume may not last; C's volume is unverified.

Evidence before signing: 90 days of members' wallet or referral data (volume per trader, days active), community activity and audience location, past partner results and a reference. Other costs to investigate: follower fee discounts (0-20%), Boost-style campaign rewards, gas subsidies, event and venue costs, content fees, fraud screening and tracking tools, compliance review (POJK 6/2026 finfluencer certification; Law 4/2026, which lets OJK act against offshore platforms serving Indonesians), translation and BD time. In the 30-day plan B goes live on day 10, so it adds about $1.33M of swaps ($533) inside the first 30 days and $2M a month after. Full table: spreadsheet, Task 2 numbers tab.
